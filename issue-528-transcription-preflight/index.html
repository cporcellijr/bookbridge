    Issue #528 — EPUB/audio transcription pre-flight (first milestone)

    Branch: codex/528-transcription-preflight, based on local dev at 9bbec982e6582266c577339984a1896e0e9cad72 (57 commits ahead of origin/dev after fetch, November 5, 2026). The implementation handoff was not deployed.

    What is measured and when

    The processing worker (src/sync_manager.py) and Auto-Forge completion (src/services/forge_service.py) call one shared, read-only evaluator (src/services/transcription_preflight.py) after extracting existing EPUB text and immediately before the full Whisper fallback. Storyteller, direct CTC, and successful SMIL routes retain their priority and do not need the advisory. The check uses catalog/provider duration; it never fetches audio, starts a probe, or transcribes to get its measurements. Evidence-write failures log a warning without interrupting processing.

    Fields: token_count (Unicode-aware letter-containing EPUB word tokens from the existing map_quality._word_tokens tokenizer), duration_seconds (positive finite value from existing source metadata), epub_tokens_per_audio_hour and assessment (uncalibrated or inconclusive). The Processing templates word each assessment through translations; no English text is stored. Missing, zero, negative, nonfinite or unreliable duration means inconclusive; empty/unspaced-script EPUB text also means inconclusive. No mismatch threshold is defined or enabled. The existing final lexical refusal, transcript caches, quarterly overlap diagnostics, audio-coverage validation and sync leader selection are untouched.

    transcription_preflights is an additive table keyed by user and book. The identity ties each acknowledgement to audio provider/source and file IDs, the ebook provider/ID and original filename, the effective measured audio duration, and a digest of the extracted EPUB text. A changed pairing, EPUB text, or effective duration/rate assessment changes the identity, which hides stale evidence and clears the old acknowledgement; unchanged evidence preserves an existing acknowledgement. Every read, write and acknowledgement requires the reader's user_books claim. When the same catalog pairing is shared, the worker publishes independent evidence to every current claimant; one reader's acknowledgement never acknowledges it for another. Dismiss acknowledges only this advisory; no approval queue is added, transcription continues automatically and ContentMatchRejected still refuses mismatches. delete_book and unlink_user_book explicitly remove the related TranscriptionPreflight rows because SQLite foreign-key enforcement is not guaranteed on established installs. Acknowledgement updates are conditional on the submitted identity and pairing key, so a concurrent refresh cannot acknowledge evidence the reader has not seen. Evidence does not go into Job.last_error. Classic and New Processing pages show the advisory; Discover links to Processing. No dependencies or settings.

    Library comparison (review follow-up)

    add_library_comparison compares each shown row with the reader's own books that are active (they passed the final content-match guard) and still have current evidence: the same tokenizer and the same catalog-duration source, which is the precondition the calibration section sets. With at least five such books, a row shows its ratio to their median plus their min–max range and count. This is a fact, not a cutoff: no threshold is applied and nothing pauses. Completed rows do not carry the advisory. The baseline only grows from books that reach the Whisper fallback after this feature shipped; books aligned earlier have no evidence rows.

    Calibration: no defensible cutoff

    Issue #528 reports 29 correctly paired Russian transcripts at 5.6k–8.6k transcript words per audio hour (median 7.4k), compared with 14.9k EPUB words/hour for the six-book omnibus (only three narrated) and 7.5k for the trimmed EPUB. Transcript word rates and EPUB token rates are not calibrated equivalents. Punctuation, morphology, Unicode normalization, EPUB notes and front/back matter, ASR omissions, silence and narration speed can all shift the values. Predominantly unspaced scripts require different segmentation. No user-verified source EPUB/audio pairs were present in the isolated checkout for establishing a same-measure, language-specific threshold. The 7.5k/14.9k examples in tests are synthetic, not the reporter's files. Both remain uncalibrated; neither is judged wrong.

    Before adding a cutoff, compare verified correct and incorrect audio/EPUB pairs with the same EPUB tokenizer, reliable audio-duration provenance, per-language distribution and measured false-positive rate. Any provisional heuristic must be opt-in and follow the complete add-setting workflow.

    Limits and deferred work

    This first milestone measures at the Whisper fallback, not at initial Discover selection. Processing refresh displays evidence when it exists (and after a run), but if Whisper begins immediately the operator may not have time to intervene. There is deliberately no blocking approval queue. An earlier Discover preview could later reuse already extracted EPUB/cache text without downloading audio or parsing every book on a page GET. A successful Storyteller/CTC/SMIL alternative does not create evidence.

    Deferred: EPUB TOC/chapter comparison, optional 15%/50%/85% audio probes, calibration-backed warnings and any pause/approval mode. A chapter count difference alone is never a reason to reject.
