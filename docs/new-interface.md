# New interface (beta)

BookBridge has two interfaces: **Classic** (the default) and **New** (optional beta). They use the same books, progress, connections, and actions. Changing the interface does not change your sync settings or reading position.

## Turn New on or off

Open **Account → Display** from the account menu and choose **Classic** or **New** under **Interface**. This works the same way on desktop and phones. Each reader has their own choice, and Classic stays the default. Classic readers can also open Display straight from the user menu.

## Library

The Library is one continuous grid of your books. Every series volume appears once, and the default sort is reading status: books and series in progress first, then not started, then finished, with the most recently synced first in each group.

- **The book you are reading now.** A card above the toolbar shows one recently synced book: its cover, where and when it last moved, the series and its number, a star rating, the percentage with time or pages left and an estimated finish date, and one progress bar that marks where each other app is. It changes only when a newer sync moves a different book. On phones it stays compact. Turn it off, or choose what it shows, on the Display page.
- **Toolbar.** Search, Filter, Sort and Select sit on one row on desktop and tablets, and on two rows on a phone.
- **Covers.** Each cover can show your progress, a tick once finished, a strip of where the book is linked (ebook, audiobook, read-along, KOReader) with the app that led its last sync lit, its rating, and its title and author. On a touch screen, tapping a format icon shows that app's position.
- **Book drawer.** Open a book to see where you are in every app, its links and its actions, including your current audiobook chapter for Audiobookshelf, BookOrbit and Grimmory audiobooks that carry chapter information.

## Display

**Account → Display** holds every look-and-feel choice for your account. Changes save as soon as you make them and show in a live preview built from your own library.

| Section | What you can choose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Interface** | Classic or New |
| **Theme** | Follow device (the default), Day or Night. The Day/Night button in the sidebar changes the same setting, and it follows your account across devices. |
| **Library style** | Cards, Face-out shelves, Spine shelves or List. Shelf finish (Match theme, Wood, Dark or Plain) applies to the two shelf styles. |
| **Book covers** | Cover size (five steps; three books per phone row by default), and whether covers show progress, the finished tick, the format strip, rating stars, and the title and author. **Current book on series stacks** shows the book you are on as a series' cover. |
| **Layout** | What the reading-now card shows: Synopsis, Book stats (listening and reading side by side) and Reading streak. Anything without data is left out. |
| **Behaviour** | Whether the reading-now card appears at all. |

## Find your way around

- **Discover:** Find audiobook/ebook pairs, ebooks without an audio match, or search for another book. Review suggestions and add them to the queue. When a book is missing its audiobook or ebook, **Get it** can request it; see [Getting missing books](user-guide.md#getting-missing-books).
- **Processing:** Work in progress, waiting and recently finished jobs, and your book requests from release to pairing. (Classic calls this the Job Queue.)
- **Stats:** Every reader can see their own reading and listening statistics.
- **Settings and Logs:** Available to admins; your account, integrations and Display page are in the user menu.

On a phone, the main sections appear in a bottom tab bar. The search control is at the top. Tap it, or press **Ctrl K** (Windows/Linux), **⌘K** (Mac) or **/** while outside a text field. Arrow keys choose results; Enter opens; Escape closes. Search only offers pages and actions your account can use.

## iPad and accessibility

Tap targets are designed for touch, and keyboard users can navigate the book drawer and search. If you're trying New on iPad Safari, check the bottom tabs, search and book drawer in portrait and landscape, and report any clipping, focus or scrolling issues. Classic remains available while New is in beta.
