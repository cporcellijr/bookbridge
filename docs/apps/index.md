# Connecting your apps

Every app connects the same way, in two places.

<div class="bb-pick bb-pick--two" markdown>
<div markdown>
Settings → Services

**Admin, once per server.** Turn the app on, enter its address and **Save Settings**. **Test** checks the connection once your login below is saved. A service switched off here is off for everyone.
</div>
<div markdown>
Account → My Integrations

**Each reader.** Your own login for the app, so everyone keeps their own progress. Click your name, choose **Account**, then **Open integrations** (readers who are not admins also have **My apps** in the sidebar). An admin can fill this in for a reader under **Settings → Users → Integrations**.
</div>
</div>

When an admin switches a service off, readers see their switch for it greyed out. Their own choice is remembered and comes back when the service is switched on again.
